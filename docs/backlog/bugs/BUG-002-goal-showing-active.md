# BUG-001 — Goal showing active status even after changing the status 
**Status:** Fixed
**Priority:** High

## Problem

Goal showing active status even after changing the status to anything else, such as archived or finished. Although it does move to other status after closing it. 

## Expected Behaviour

Goal will show the correct status after it is changed. 

## Actual Behaviour

Goal showing active status even after changing the status to anything else, such as archived or finished. 

## Reproduction

1. Create a goal
2. Change the status 
3. Goal showing active status even after changing the status to anything else, such as archived or finished. Although it does move to other status after closing it. 

## Notes

Please check the vice versa scnearios after being fixed.
