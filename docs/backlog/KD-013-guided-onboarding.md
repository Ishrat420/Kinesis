### KD-013 — Guided Onboarding

**Status:** Idea
**Priority:** Low
**Tags:** UX / UI, Needs Research

## Summary

Introduce a short guided onboarding experience that teaches users how Kinesis works without requiring them to discover the architecture themselves.

Take inspiration from products such as Capacities while keeping onboarding specific to Kinesis.

Potential introduction:

```text
Documents
Keep important records organised and know when they need attention.

Goals
Turn intentions into milestones and measurable progress.

Finance
Understand your financial position without becoming a budgeting app.

Relationships
Be intentional about the relationships that matter to you.
```

Onboarding should also introduce core Kinesis concepts such as Modules, linking, reminders and the dashboard.

## Requirements

Keep onboarding short, visual and skippable.

The objective is to help users understand **how to think in Kinesis**, not provide a feature tour of every button.
